# Malaysian media law: status snapshot

Checked: 22 October 2026. This is a starting point, not a source to cite.
Confirm anything that may have changed after this date against the Federal
Gazette (lom.agc.gov.my), Parliament (parlimen.gov.my) or MCMC, and cite
the primary source in the report.

| Law | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Communications and Multimedia (Amendment) Act 2025 | In force 11 February 2025 (except ss. 92 and 112 of the amending Act) | Amends the CMA 1998 (Act 588). In s. 233(1)(a), "offensive" became "grossly offensive"; s. 233 also now covers content sent with intent to commit fraud or dishonesty offences. Check penalties and the current wording of s. 233 before quoting it. |
| Online Safety Act 2025 (Act 866) | In force 1 January 2026 | Royal Assent 6 May 2025; gazetted 22 May 2025. Applies to licensed application service and content application service providers; secondary sources say social media and messaging platforms with 8 million or more Malaysian users are deemed licensees (verify). Regulator: MCMC. Check subsidiary legislation (codes, age rules) for anything in force after the checked date. |
| Malaysian Media Council Act 2025 (Act 868) | In force 14 June 2025 | Passed February 2025. Council established July 2025. A self-regulatory body for journalists: code of conduct, complaints, standards. It is not an enforcement agency. |
| Sedition Act 1948 | In force (not re-checked for 2025–2026 changes) | Upheld against Art. 10 challenge in *Public Prosecutor v Azmi Sharom* [2015] 6 MLJ 751 (FC). Check for any amendment or repeal announcement. |
| Printing Presses and Publications Act 1984 | In force (not re-checked for 2025–2026 changes) | Licensing and the Home Ministry's (KDN) power to prohibit publications. Verify current status and any reform before citing. |
| Penal Code ss. 298A, 499–500, 504, 505 | Not re-checked | Religious-harmony, defamation, insult and public-mischief offences often charged alongside CMA s. 233. Verify each section's current status, including any constitutional challenge, before citing. |
