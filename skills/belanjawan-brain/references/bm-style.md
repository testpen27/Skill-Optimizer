# Bahasa Melayu style guide for citizen-facing text

All questions, options, results and advisories are read by ordinary Malaysians. Use standard, respectful, formal-but-plain Bahasa Melayu, as a government information page would. When unsure, use the term the budget speech itself uses.

## Terms to avoid (and what to use)

| Avoid | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| makan gaji | Pekerja sektor swasta | Colloquial and can read as belittling |
| freelance | pekerja bebas | Use the Malay term |
| siak | tok siak | The speech's own wording |
| masalah pembelajaran | kurang upaya pembelajaran | Official term |
| dari RM…, dari perniagaan… | daripada RM…, daripada… | "dari" is for place and time; "daripada" for amounts, sources and comparisons |
| Automatik untuk penerima STR. | Diberikan secara automatik kepada penerima STR. | Avoid verbless fragments |
| Tidak mahu nyatakan | Tidak mahu menyatakan | Needs the meN- verb form |
| TT2026 | Tahun Taksiran 2026 | Spell out abbreviations for the public |
| dll. | dan lain-lain / dan sebagainya | Write in full |
| 8 jenis, 5 tahun pertama | lapan jenis, lima tahun pertama | Write small numbers in words within prose |
| janda / duda as a category label | Tiada pasangan (belum berkahwin, bercerai atau kematian pasangan) | Neutral and respectful |
| teksi atau kereta sewa (after "Pemandu atau pemilik") | teksi, termasuk kereta sewa | Avoid a double "atau" |
| nak, kat, dorang, tu | (formal equivalents) | Colloquial |

## Conventions

- Capitalise **Kerajaan** when it means the Government of Malaysia, as MOF documents do.
- Write money as **RM1,200**, and **RM1 juta** only for loan or guarantee limits a person can receive, never for government allocations.
- Write ranges as **"RM2,501 hingga RM5,000"** and **"21 hingga 59 tahun"**. Use **"dan ke bawah" / "ke atas"** for thresholds.
- Keep official programme names as published, even if English: **Step-Up Financing**, **MySiswa Job on Campus**, **K-Youth**, **BUDI95**.
- Loan words are acceptable when they are the common term (**vape**, **CCTV**, **ETF**). Add the Malay term in brackets on first use where one exists, e.g. **cerut kecil (cigarillo)**.
- Use **hamil** for pregnancy in labels (more formal than "mengandung").
- Question text should be a full question ("Berapakah…?", "Apakah…?", "Adakah…?"), not a fragment ("Jantina anda?").
- The `why` labels are short noun phrases, e.g. "Berumur 40 tahun ke atas".

## Tone

- **Plain and neutral.** No marketing language ("hebat", "terbaik") and no political praise. State what the person gets, who qualifies and what to do.
- **Honest about uncertainty.** Use "Semak kelayakan" or "Mungkin layak" rather than implying a guarantee.
- **Neutral on "kesan" items** (price or duty increases). State the change and the date, and point to help (e.g. quit-smoking aids) where it exists. Don't moralise.
