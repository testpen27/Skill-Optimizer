## Notes
- **Where "rural" is covered.** Very few Malaysian studies put "rural" in the title, abstract or keywords. Requiring that word would lose most of the relevant papers. The niche block therefore covers rural Malaysia through the mostly rural states, Orang Asli, FELDA and Klinik Desa. Apply "rural setting" and "aged 60+" as inclusion criteria at screening. To force "rural" into the search anyway, add this block before the closing bracket of the narrow string, inside `TITLE-ABS-KEY(...)` or `TS=(...)`. It passed my syntax check for both databases:
```
AND (rural OR village* OR kampung* OR "remote area*" OR "luar bandar" OR underserved OR "hard-to-reach")
```
- **Ages.** No search term can enforce 60+ reliably, so check age at screening. I left out the bare word `aged`, because Scopus indexes "Aged" on almost every clinical paper.
- **Niche, not affiliation.** Concept 4 filters on what the paper is about, not where the authors work.
- **Checker result.** All strings passed `search_tool.js`. Its only warning was that Web of Science does not match plurals inside quotes. I truncated the phrases that can be plural (for example `"older adult*"`, `"intention* to use"`). The rest are non-count nouns or names ("virtual care", "digital literacy", "Orang Asli") and need no change.
