# Questions before starting

You already have four papers and want a Literature Review Matrix (LRM) PDF of them. Before I build it, please confirm or correct these so the matrix is filtered and labelled properly:

1. **Field or discipline:** which field is this for (for example Education, Psychology, Information Systems)? I ask even when the topic looks clear, because the field decides which Scimago subject category I use for each journal's quartile.
2. **Working title or research question:** what is your study's title or main question, so I can check how each paper fits your constructs?
3. **Population or context:** who or what are you studying (for example undergraduates, secondary school pupils, MOOC learners)?
4. **Niche:** any country, region, sector or platform focus (for example Malaysia)?
5. **Year range:** which publication years should the matrix cover? If you have no preference, I suggest the last five calendar years including this one, 2022 to 2026. Several ranges are fine.
6. **Review type:** is this for a systematic, scoping, bibliometric or narrative review, or just background for a proposal?
7. **Databases:** does your university give you Scopus, Web of Science, or neither?
8. **What you want at the end:** just the LRM of these four papers, or should I also find more papers, build search strings, or run a PRISMA search?
9. **Where to save:** should I save the files in a folder you name, or in your Downloads folder?
