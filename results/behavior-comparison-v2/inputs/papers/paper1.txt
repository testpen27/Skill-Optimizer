Title: Gamified quizzes and intrinsic motivation among Malaysian undergraduates
Authors: Tan B., Rahman S. (2023)
Journal: Journal of Asian Education Studies
DOI: 10.1/a
Introduction: Examines whether gamified quizzes raise intrinsic motivation.
Method: Cross-sectional survey, 320 undergraduates at two public universities, PLS-SEM.
Results: Gamified quiz use predicted intrinsic motivation (beta = 0.38, p < .001).
Discussion: Supports self-determination theory; recommends autonomy-supportive design.
Framework: Self-Determination Theory
