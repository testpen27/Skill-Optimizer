Check every sentence against the quotes of its own column (and any other column's quotes if the sentence is clearly about the same fact).

Reply with ONE JSON object only, no fence, no extra text:
{"verdicts":[{"id":"methodology.1","verdict":"keep|cut|narrow","reason":"one short sentence","support":"for keep: the exact quote that supports it; for cut or narrow: the exact words of the sentence that are unsupported"}]}
