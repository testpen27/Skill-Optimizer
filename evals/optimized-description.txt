Use this skill for academic database searching on Scopus (also called "Opus"), Web of Science (WoS) and Google Scholar. Turn a research title, topic or review type (systematic, scoping, bibliometric) into Boolean search strings, keywords or a concept table. Run the strings and record hit counts, download the free full-text papers found, or produce a search strategy report or PRISMA flow diagram. Also use it whenever someone pastes a search string they already wrote and asks about it: is it too long, too broad or narrow, returning zero results, an "invalid query", or needs shortening, fixing or adapting to another database. Covers field tags (TS=, TITLE-ABS-KEY), AND/OR/NOT, truncation, wildcards, phrase quotes, country niches, year ranges, and Malay or other local-language strings. Not for screening abstracts, writing the review, literature matrices, picking manuscript keywords, or general Boolean, regex or SQL help.
