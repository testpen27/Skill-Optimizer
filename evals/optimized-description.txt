Use this skill for anything involving Boolean search queries for academic literature databases: Scopus (users may say "Opus"), Web of Science (WoS) and Google Scholar. Build ready-to-paste search strings, keywords or a concept table from a research title, topic, question or SLR title. Also use it to debug, fix, shorten, widen or narrow a search the user already wrote, including when a database rejects it with "invalid query", a syntax error, zero results, or too many or too few hits. Handles field tags (TS=, TITLE-ABS-KEY, PY=), AND/OR/NOT logic, truncation (*), wildcards (?), phrase quotes, country niches, date limits, Malay or other local-language strings, and exporting the search strategy as a Word file. Not for screening abstracts, general Boolean or SQL help, or finding specific papers.
