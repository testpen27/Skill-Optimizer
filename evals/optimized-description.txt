Pre-research for literature reviews, from topic to papers. Use it when someone gives a research title, topic or question and wants literature found or organised: Boolean search strings for Scopus (users may say "Opus"), Web of Science or Google Scholar; a systematic search run with hit counts; a PRISMA 2020 flow diagram, screening record or search strategy report; the papers downloaded (open access or via their library) into a folder; or a Literature Review Matrix (LRM, synthesis matrix, RRL) with IMRAD columns. Also use it whenever someone pastes a search string and asks about it (invalid query, syntax error, too long for Scholar, zero or too many results, convert to another database), or asks to classify, rank or tabulate papers they already have by journal quartile (Q1-Q4) or method. Covers field tags, AND/OR/NOT, truncation, country niches, year ranges and local-language strings. Not for writing the review, summarising one paper, citation formatting, or general Boolean, regex or SQL help.
