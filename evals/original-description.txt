Build ready-to-paste Boolean search strings for finding academic papers in Scopus, Web of Science and Google Scholar. Use this whenever the user gives a research title, topic, research question or SLR title and wants search strings, keywords, a Boolean search, a concept table, or a way to find papers on Scopus (they may say "Opus", "Scopus" or "WoS"), Web of Science or Google Scholar. Also use it when they ask to fix, shorten, widen or narrow an existing search string, when a search returned too many or too few results, or when they want the search strategy as a Word file. Covers AND/OR logic, truncation (*), wildcards (?), phrase searching, a niche such as one country, date limits, and Malay or other local-language strings.
